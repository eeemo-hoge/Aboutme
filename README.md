## Greetings and My Philosophy

Hello.
I’m a programmer who’s mainly active online.

I want to create software that makes everyday life just a little bit easier.
What I value most is simplicity and lightness.
Everything I create is free and aligns with the GNU philosophy.

If asked, “Why do you code?”—of course, part of the reason is to build a portfolio for potential employers.
But the main reason is that I love the atmosphere of the early Heisei era. I’m drawn to the “true nature” of hardware and software (in my opinion, at least), and I want to bring that vision to life.
I’d be happy to find work in that field, but even if that proves difficult, I plan to keep enjoying this on my own.
